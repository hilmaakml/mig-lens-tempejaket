A real case reported by detikJogja. The name and details are quoted from that reporting.

Illustration. Not a photograph of the person interviewed.

Herlambang, aged 23

Kulon Progo, Special Region of Yogyakarta

Timeline according to the reporting

  1. 1Late August 2024Applied to work as a factory operator in Taiwan. The offer later changed to shop keeper in Thailand.
  2. 2Before departurePaid an initial fee of Rp25 million and was sent abroad very quickly on a visitor passport — not a work visa.
  3. 3Around 3 September 2024Arrived not in Thailand but in Cambodia, and was forced to work as an online scammer targeting Indonesians.
  4. 4Until November 2025Held for about a year and subjected to violence. He escaped with ten other people and returned to Indonesia in November 2025.

Yang pertama itu harus jelas dulu dari PT ataupun lembaganya. Dicari tahu dulu.

Herlambang, speaking to detikJogja

What can be learned

  • An offer that changes country or role before departure is something to have clarified in writing, not simply followed.
  • Travelling on a visitor passport in order to work means the visa type does not match the job. Ask which visa applies, and on what legal basis, before paying.
  • A large fee up front combined with a rushed departure leaves little time to check the company, the contract, and where the money is going.
